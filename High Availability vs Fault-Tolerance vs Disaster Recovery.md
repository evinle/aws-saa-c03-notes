- Aims to ensure
	- An agreed level of operational performance - usually uptime
	- For a higher than normal period
	- Not about user experience
	- About maximizing a system's online time, minimizing outages
	- Even if the user needs to relogin - minor user disruption - this is okay

## Fault Tolerance
- Is a property that enables
	- A system to continue operating properly 
	- Even in the event of the failure of some (1 or more faults within) of its components
	- Even while they are present or being fixed
	- Continue operating through a failure without affecting users
- Can be expensive, much more complex to implement
- ![[Pasted image 20260425192530.png]]
- Redundancy is very important

## Disaster Recovery
- Set of policies, tools and procedures 
- To enable the recovery or continuation of vital infra and systems
- Following a natural or human-induced disaster

Should always have pre-planning
- Staffing
- Documentation
- Standby-premises
- Offsite backups
- Periodic DR testing
